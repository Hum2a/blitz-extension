## Full Description (max 16,000 chars)

**Stop sending the same CV to every job. Start getting callbacks.**

Blitz is the AI-powered job application assistant that lives in your browser. It reads the job description, rewrites your CV to match it, writes a tailored cover letter, and fills in the application — all in under a minute. You review and approve everything before it touches the form.

---

### How it works

1. **Browse a job listing** on LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, or Workday
2. **Blitz detects the role** and generates a tailored CV + cover letter in seconds
3. **Review and approve** — edit anything you want before it fills the form
4. **Blitz applies for you** — typing like a human, protecting your account

That's it. No copy-pasting. No rewriting the same summary for the hundredth time. No forgetting to swap out the company name in the cover letter.

---

### Features

**AI-tailored applications**
Every CV and cover letter is rewritten specifically for the role you're applying to. Skills reordered. Summary rewritten to mirror the job description. Bullet points adjusted to highlight what the employer actually cares about. Nothing fabricated — just your real experience, presented better.

**Smart screening questions**
LinkedIn and Indeed often ask screening questions: right to work, salary expectations, years of experience, notice period. Blitz answers these automatically using your profile, so you never get caught out by a question you forgot to fill.

**Match scoring**
Before you approve, Blitz shows you a match score — how well your CV fits the role — plus a plain-English explanation. Apply to the right roles, skip the wrong ones.

**Application tracker**
Every application you send is logged automatically. Track status (applied, interview, offer, rejected), see which CVs and cover letters you used, and spot patterns in what's working.

**Your data stays yours**
Blitz uses your own AI API key (Anthropic or OpenAI). Your CV, your key, your data — none of it passes through our servers. Everything is stored locally in your browser.

**Human-speed form filling**
Blitz types and clicks like a person, not a bot. Randomised delays, realistic behaviour. Your account stays safe.
